# PUENTE demo guide

**Purpose:** prepare the course demo after a real URL is available. The course asks for about three minutes of walkthrough plus 30 seconds on what changed your mind. Do not describe this guide as a recorded video.

1. **0:00–0:30 — Problem.** “A new work option can look attractive before we account for the first-pay gap. PUENTE helps someone look at their essential costs and next step.” State that the prototype is a simulated exercise with fictional inputs.
2. **0:30–1:10 — Start the flow.** Show the startup landing page, select “Explorar mi ruta” and “Cargar ejemplo.” Explain that the example is fictional and no real name or financial data is required.
3. **1:10–2:00 — Calculation.** Show the five daily records, weekly household minimum, retained hours and transition costs. Explain the 14-day floor, lowest positive-day reserve and that no unconfirmed salary is added during the bridge. Run the calculation.
4. **2:00–2:40 — State and fallback.** Read the state, reason and suggested next action. Change an input, rerun and explain that a human decides; a source field does not prove an employer or route is verified.
5. **2:40–3:00 — Saved output.** Save and recover the fictional example in the browser or download JSON. Say plainly that this version does not yet save to a server.
6. **Final 30 seconds — Reflection.** Describe a decision you actually changed after the real review or build. A candidate technical learning to consider, if it reflects your experience: the old reserve included a zero day and the new rule uses the lowest positive day. Do not claim a deployment, user interview or measured outcome that did not happen.

Before recording, confirm that the live URL, audio and screen recording work, and check the final video's duration and playback.
