# PUENTE Personas and Evidence

**Status:** synthetic design and testing inputs. No person below was interviewed for this document, no offer or employer was contacted, and none of the scenarios demonstrates the course's real-user or 10:1 gates.

## Primary persona — Luis, synthetic

| Field | Scenario assumption |
|---|---|
| Profile | Luis, age 20, Ecatepec; works informally selling packaged candy to contribute to household costs. Invented from the Week 4 exercise. |
| Device and use | A phone-first Spanish-language flow; may stop and resume after gathering information. Device or reading ability is a design assumption, not an observed trait. |
| Trigger | Considering a formal job or training route while income and first-pay timing are uncertain. |
| Current option | Continue candy sales while asking for written details. The time or expense of checking real opportunities has not been observed. |
| Synthetic cash | MXN 1,800 available; MXN 1,200 weekly household floor; MXN 2,400 for 14 days. The baseline gap before reserve and transition costs is at least MXN 600 if no retained income is counted. |
| Route example | Hypothetical monthly pay MXN 9,500 and first pay after 14 days; no employer, terms or availability verified. Future pay is not counted during the bridge without dated confirmation. |
| Need | Understand cash protection, missing route terms, one low-cost next action and a fallback without being scored or pushed into a choice. |

**Important arithmetic:** the MXN 600 gap is a baseline comparison of current cash to the floor, before a reserve, costs or any retained income. Once a selling log and retained hours are entered, the route-specific bridge must be recalculated; do not mechanically reuse `−600` as the final route margin.

## Supporting actor — advisor, synthetic

An advisor helps explain the calculation, checks a route's source and date, and records a missing term or named owner. This role exists as a possible workflow; no partner, staffing commitment or capacity has been verified. The user approves any handoff and makes the decision.

## What research exists and what does not

- The Week 4 PUENTE packet and demo contain a fictional Luis and synthetic product checks. Their existence demonstrates a design, not a real person's experience.
- The Week 12 simulated interview annex is invented. It is not a transcript of a real participant and must never be used to satisfy the requirement for a real person in October.
- Available evidence does not establish real employment terms, employer acceptance, payer, advisor capacity, 10:1 superiority or conversion to work.
- Obtain a real participant's current workaround and episode date with consent before treating the need as validated; protect identifying information and avoid promises of a job.

## Synthetic persona test plan

Open a fresh conversation and clearly identify Luis as simulated. Walk through screenshots in this order: (1) setup and privacy boundary, (2) cash inputs, (3) route details and evidence, (4) status and reason, (5) fallback, (6) saved or exported result. Ask the simulated person to say what they think each screen means, what they would do and where they would stop. Log screen, confusion, severity, proposed fix and any actual retest. Do not report observations, fixes, dates or retests until they happen.

**Success for the simulation:** the person can identify the protected amount, distinguish missing evidence from an insufficient bridge, name the next step and keep the fallback. This is a design review, not a substitute for a real-user interview.
